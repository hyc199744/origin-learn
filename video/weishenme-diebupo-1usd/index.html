🎯 核心内容（本站整理，非 AI 生成）🎯 Key points (compiled by us, not AI-generated)

  • 价格在 AMM 底池里由两种资产的比例决定，储备越厚支撑越强。In an AMM pool, price is set by the ratio of the two assets; the deeper the reserves, the stronger the support.
  • 所谓“跌不破”是机制上的软底，取决于国库真实储备，不是硬承诺。The so-called "can't drop below" is a mechanism-based soft floor that depends on the treasury's real reserves — not a hard promise.
  • 要判断托底是否可靠，必须自己上链核实国库里的真实外部资产。To judge whether the floor is reliable, you must verify the treasury's real external assets on-chain yourself.
