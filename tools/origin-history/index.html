质押、sLGNS 与 RebaseStaking, sLGNS and Rebase

用户把 LGNS 质押进质押池，1:1 得到质押凭证 sLGNS；协议按固定周期做 Rebase，把新铸的 LGNS 分给质押者，于是每个人的 sLGNS 余额会自动增长。这套源自 OlympusDAO 的 (3,3) 玩法，鼓励大家长期质押、少频繁买卖。Users stake LGNS into the staking pool and receive the staking receipt sLGNS 1:1; the protocol runs Rebase on a fixed cycle, distributing newly minted LGNS to stakers, so everyone's sLGNS balance grows automatically. This (3,3) design, derived from OlympusDAO, encourages long-term staking over frequent trading.

Rebase 的节奏一路优化，从早期较长的周期逐步加密到如今每 6 小时一次（Anubis 上 8h→7h→6h 的逐段演变见卷三）。围绕它，活期质押与 360 天、600 天等长期产品陆续成形，构成起源「赚数量」的复利体系。要提醒的是——Rebase 增加的是 LGNS 的数量，一枚 LGNS 值多少钱由市场与底池另行决定，数量增长和美元收益是两回事。The Rebase cadence was optimized over time, tightening from longer early cycles to once every 6 hours today (the 8h→7h→6h evolution on Anubis is in Volume III). Around it, flexible staking and long-term products of 360 and 600 days took shape, forming Origin's compounding "earn-by-quantity" system. A caution: Rebase increases the quantity of LGNS; what one LGNS is worth is decided separately by the market and the pool — quantity growth and dollar returns are two different things.

先把名词一次说白。债券，本质是一张“打折买币券”：你把稳定币（DAI／USDT）交给国库，国库按低于市价的折扣价把 LGNS 卖给你。但币不当场全给——它分批解锁，这段等待叫归属期（vesting）。你让渡了即时流动性，换来的是折价空间。First, the terms in plain words. A bond is essentially a “discounted coin voucher”: you give the treasury stablecoins (DAI/USDT), and it sells you LGNS at a discount below market. But the coins aren't all given at once — they unlock in batches, and this wait is the vesting period. You give up instant liquidity in exchange for the discount margin.

一笔债券里藏着三个数：折扣（比市价便宜多少）、归属期（多久拿全）、以及由这两者推出的年化收益。折扣越大、锁得越久，等待期里的价格波动就越归你承担——这正是折扣要补偿你的东西。A bond hides three numbers: the discount (how much cheaper than market), the vesting period (how long to fully receive), and the annualized return derived from the two. The bigger the discount and the longer the lock, the more of the waiting-period price swings you bear — which is exactly what the discount compensates you for.

对协议而言，债券是国库真正的“造血管”。它把散在市场里的真金白银换成锁进国库的长期储备（业内叫协议自有价值 PCV）；相比在二级市场直接卖币，债券把抛压拉平到整个归属期里，国库拿到的是稳定、可预期的现金流。For the protocol, bonds are the treasury's true 'blood vessel'. They turn real money scattered in the market into long-term reserves locked in the treasury (industry term: protocol-owned value, PCV); compared with selling coins directly on the secondary market, bonds flatten sell pressure across the whole vesting period, giving the treasury steady, predictable cash flow.

这套机制不是起源发明的，而是 OHM 系协议的经典打法。起源把它搬上 Polygon，跑通后又延伸到自建的 Anubis 链。链上看，恰好是清晰的三代：This mechanism wasn't invented by Origin but is a classic play of OHM-family protocols. Origin brought it to Polygon and, once proven, extended it to its self-built Anubis chain. On-chain, it forms exactly three clear generations:

第一代·储备债券（2024-03）：DAI、USDT、WBTC 三张券同一天部署，直接收硬通货。这是国库的第一桶“外部真金”。Gen 1 · reserve bonds (2024-03): three vouchers — DAI, USDT, WBTC — deployed the same day, taking hard currency directly. This was the treasury's first bucket of “external real gold”.
