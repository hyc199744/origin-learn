LGNS 是什么？What is LGNS?

⏱ 7 分钟7 min
学习目标：学完你能说清 LGNS 是什么类型的代币、精度几位、靠什么机制，以及去哪查它的合约。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to say what kind of token LGNS is, how many decimals it has, what mechanism drives it, and where to look up its contract.
大白话In plain wordsLGNS 是起源生态的核心代币，属于「算法代币」（不是稳定币），用一套叫 (3,3) 的玩法鼓励大家长期质押持有，而不是频繁买卖。LGNS is the core token of the ORIGIN ecosystem. It's an algorithmic token (not a stablecoin) and uses a game called (3,3) to encourage everyone to stake and hold for the long term, rather than trade frequently.

LGNS（朗基努斯 Longinus）是起源生态的核心代币，源自 OlympusDAO 的分叉设计，属于算法代币。它最特别的一点：精度是 9 位（decimals=9），不是常见的 18 位——链上读到的原始数字要除以 10⁹ 才是真实枚数，很多人按 18 位算会差一千倍。LGNS (Longinus) is the core token of the ORIGIN ecosystem, forked from OlympusDAO's design, and it's an algorithmic token. Its most special trait: it has 9 decimals (decimals=9), not the common 18 — the raw number you read on-chain must be divided by 10⁹ to get the real count. Many people calculate it as 18 decimals and end up off by a thousand times.

它的核心机制是 (3,3) + 质押 rebase：协议每 6 小时增发一次新币，按比例分给质押者，所以你质押后持有的数量会自动慢慢变多。这就是大家常说的「高年化」的来源。Its core mechanism is (3,3) + staking rebase: the protocol mints new tokens every 6 hours and distributes them proportionally to stakers, so the amount you hold after staking slowly grows on its own. This is the source of the so-called "high APY".

它同时部署在两条链上：It's deployed on two chains at the same time:

  • Polygon: 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01
  • Anubis: 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8
你可以在 PolygonScan 或 Anubis 浏览器上独立核实。You can independently verify it on PolygonScan or the Anubis block explorer.

理性提醒：数量变多 ≠ 购买力变多。质押奖励本质是增发，你的真实收益要同时看 LGNS 的币价和协议的真实储备，不能只盯着「币的个数在涨」。任何收益都请自己上链核实、量力而行。A reality check: more tokens ≠ more purchasing power. Staking rewards are essentially minting; your real return depends on both the LGNS price and the protocol's real reserves — don't just stare at "the token count going up". Verify any return on-chain yourself, and only invest what you can afford (DYOR).

案例Example链上查到某地址 LGNS 余额显示 5000000000，别慌——LGNS 是 9 位精度，除以 10⁹ = 5 枚，不是 50 亿枚。You look up an address on-chain and its LGNS balance shows 5000000000 — don't panic. LGNS has 9 decimals, so divide by 10⁹ = 5 tokens, not 5 billion.

解析：LGNS 是 9 位精度，OlympusDAO 分叉传统，不是常见的 18 位。Explanation: LGNS has 9 decimals — an OlympusDAO fork tradition — not the common 18.

解析：LGNS 是算法代币，不是稳定币。Explanation: LGNS is an algorithmic token, not a stablecoin.

❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

LGNS 的精度为什么是 9 位不是 18 位？Why does LGNS have 9 decimals instead of 18?

这是 OlympusDAO 分叉代币的传统设计。计算金额时要除以 10⁹，按 18 位算会差一千倍，务必注意。It's a traditional design of OlympusDAO fork tokens. When calculating amounts you must divide by 10⁹; treating it as 18 decimals is off by a thousand times, so be careful.

LGNS 的合约地址是多少？What is the LGNS contract address?

Polygon 链是 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01，Anubis 链是 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8，都可在区块浏览器独立核实。On Polygon it's 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01; on the Anubis chain it's 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8. Both can be independently verified on a block explorer.

LGNS 质押的高年化是真赚钱吗？Does LGNS staking's high APY really make money?

高年化本质是协议增发让你持有的数量变多，但数量涨不等于购买力涨。真实收益要同时看币价和协议真实储备，请自己上链核实。High APY is essentially the protocol minting so the amount you hold grows, but more tokens doesn't mean more purchasing power. Real returns depend on both the price and the protocol's real reserves — verify it on-chain yourself.
