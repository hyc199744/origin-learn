DAO 是什么？What is a DAO?

⏱ 6 分钟6 min
学习目标：学完你能说清 DAO 是什么、Awake DAO 在起源里干什么，以及投票权从哪来。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to say what a DAO is, what Awake DAO does in ORIGIN, and where voting power comes from.
大白话In plain wordsDAO 就是「大家一起投票说了算」的组织，没有老板拍板，改什么、花什么钱都靠社区提案和投票决定。A DAO is an organization where "everyone votes and the vote decides". There's no boss making the call — what to change and how to spend money are all decided by community proposals and votes.

DAO（去中心化自治组织）是一种「靠社区投票来决策」的组织形式。没有传统公司里的老板，规则和资金的动用要经过提案 → 讨论 → 投票 → 执行的流程，理论上让社区共同治理。A DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) is a form of organization that "decides by community vote". There's no boss like in a traditional company; changing rules and moving funds go through a proposal → discussion → vote → execution process, so that in theory the community governs together.

起源生态的治理发生在 Awake DAO。社区成员可以就「改哪个参数、国库的钱怎么用、上不上某个新功能」等提出提案，再由有投票权的人投票决定。Governance in the ORIGIN ecosystem happens in Awake DAO. Community members can put forward proposals on things like "which parameter to change, how to spend treasury funds, whether to ship a new feature", and those with voting power then decide by vote.

关键一点：起源里的投票权来自治理代币 GLGNS 和治理 NFT，不是你持有或质押多少 LGNS 就自动有投票权。这也是 LGNS（核心代币）和 GLGNS（治理代币）分工的原因。A key point: in ORIGIN, voting power comes from the governance token GLGNS and governance NFTs, not automatically from how much LGNS you hold or stake. This is exactly why LGNS (the core token) and GLGNS (the governance token) have separate roles.

提醒：DAO 是否「真去中心化」要看细节——投票门槛、提案是否真的执行、票权是否高度集中在少数地址。这些都可以在链上核实，别只听「我们是 DAO 治理」这句口号。A reminder: whether a DAO is "truly decentralized" comes down to details — the voting threshold, whether proposals actually get executed, and whether voting power is highly concentrated in a few addresses. These can all be verified on-chain; don't just take the slogan "we're DAO-governed" at face value.

案例Example社区想把某个奖励比例调低，正规做法是有人在 Awake DAO 发起提案、公示讨论、持 GLGNS/治理 NFT 的人投票，通过后才执行——而不是某个人私下改掉。Say the community wants to lower a reward ratio. The proper way is for someone to start a proposal in Awake DAO, open it for discussion, have GLGNS / governance NFT holders vote, and only execute it after it passes — not have one person quietly change it.

📝 本节测验📝 Quiz

1. DAO 最核心的特征是？1. What is the core feature of a DAO?

解析：DAO 靠社区投票治理，不是单人拍板。Explanation: a DAO is governed by community votes, not one person's decision.

2. 起源的社区治理发生在哪里？2. Where does ORIGIN's community governance take place?

解析：Awake DAO 是起源的治理组织。Explanation: Awake DAO is ORIGIN's governance organization.

3. 起源里的投票权来自？3. Where does voting power in ORIGIN come from?

解析：投票权来自 GLGNS 和治理 NFT，不是直接来自持有/质押 LGNS。Explanation: voting power comes from GLGNS and governance NFTs, not directly from holding/staking LGNS.

4. 一个规范的治理流程是？4. What is a proper governance process?

解析：规范流程是提案、讨论、投票、执行。Explanation: the proper process is propose, discuss, vote, execute.

5. 判断一个 DAO 是否真去中心化，应该看？5. To judge whether a DAO is truly decentralized, you should look at?

解析：要看可核实的链上细节，而不是宣传口号。Explanation: look at verifiable on-chain details, not marketing slogans.

❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

起源的 DAO 叫什么？投票权怎么来？What is ORIGIN's DAO called, and where does voting power come from?

叫 Awake DAO。投票权来自治理代币 GLGNS 和治理 NFT，而不是直接来自持有或质押 LGNS。It's called Awake DAO. Voting power comes from the governance token GLGNS and governance NFTs, not directly from holding or staking LGNS.

DAO 治理就一定去中心化吗？Does DAO governance always mean decentralized?

不一定。要看投票门槛、提案是否真正执行、票权是否高度集中在少数地址等细节，这些都能在链上核实。Not necessarily. It depends on details like the voting threshold, whether proposals actually get executed, and whether voting power is highly concentrated in a few addresses — all of which can be verified on-chain.
