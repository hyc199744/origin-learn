什么是收益模型？What is a Yield Model?

⏱ 7 分钟7 min
学习目标：学完你能看懂一个项目的收益是怎么产生的、可不可持续，学会区分「真实利润驱动」和「靠增发或新人接盘」两类模型，形成基本的风险直觉。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to see how a project's yield is produced and whether it's sustainable, tell apart "real-profit-driven" models from "minting- or newcomer-funded" ones, and build a basic instinct for risk.
大白话In plain words收益模型就是「这个项目发给你的钱到底从哪来」。是靠真本事赚来的，还是靠不停印新币、拿后面人的钱补前面人，一问就见分晓。A yield model is simply "where the money this project pays you actually comes from". Whether it's earned by real skill, or by endlessly printing new tokens and using later people's money to cover earlier ones — one question makes it clear.

收益模型，指一个协议产生并分配收益的整套逻辑。看懂它，核心就一句话：你的收益究竟从哪来？钱的来源，决定了这份收益能撑多久。A yield model is the whole logic by which a protocol produces and distributes yield. Understanding it comes down to one question: where does your yield actually come from? The source of the money decides how long that yield can last.

大致可分两类：Roughly two types:

  • 真实价值驱动：收益来自手续费、真实业务、外部真金白银的收入Real-value-driven: yield comes from fees, real business, and genuine external income
  • 增发或接盘驱动：收益主要靠凭空印新币，或靠不断有新资金进场补给老人Minting- or exit-liquidity-driven: yield mainly relies on printing new tokens out of thin air, or on a constant stream of new money funding earlier participants
后者越往后越难维持。The latter gets harder to sustain the longer it runs.

有几个危险信号要警惕：承诺固定高收益、收益远超协议真实赚钱能力、只有拉更多人进来才能兑付、储备里没有多少真实资产。收益越是脱离真实利润，越可能是击鼓传花。A few danger signs to watch for: a promised fixed high return, yield far beyond the protocol's real earning ability, payouts that only work if you pull more people in, and reserves holding little real asset. The more the yield detaches from real profit, the more likely it's a game of pass-the-parcel.

判断方法就一个字：查。用链上数据面板核实协议的真实储备和收入，用收益计算器把账算清，别被年化数字和宣传话术带节奏。收益要同时看币价和真实储备，务必 DYOR。There's one method: check. Use the on-chain dashboard to verify the protocol's real reserves and income, use the yield calculator to do the math, and don't let the APY figure or marketing talk set your pace. Judge yield by both the token price and the real reserves — always DYOR.

案例Example两个都标着高收益的项目，A 的收益来自实打实的手续费分成，储备里有真资产;B 的收益全靠增发新币加拉新人接盘，账上没多少真钱。表面数字差不多，但 B 一旦没有新资金进来就可能崩，这就是收益模型的差别。Two projects both advertise high yield. A's yield comes from solid fee-sharing and its reserves hold real assets; B's yield relies entirely on minting new tokens plus new people providing exit liquidity, with little real money on the books. The headline numbers look similar, but B can collapse the moment new money stops coming in — that's the difference a yield model makes.

📝 本节测验📝 Quiz

1. 看懂收益模型，最核心的问题是什么？1. What is the most central question when understanding a yield model?

解析：钱从哪来决定收益能否持续，是判断的核心。Explanation: where the money comes from decides whether the yield can last — that's the core of the judgment.

2. 下面哪种属于「真实价值驱动」的收益来源？2. Which of the following is a "real-value-driven" source of yield?

解析：手续费、真实业务收入属于可持续的真实价值来源。Explanation: fees and real business income are sustainable, real-value sources.

3. 哪个是收益模型的危险信号？3. Which is a danger sign in a yield model?

解析：脱离真实盈利能力的固定高收益，往往难以持续。Explanation: a fixed high return detached from real earning ability is usually unsustainable.

4. 「靠不断有新资金进场补给老人」的模型问题在哪？4. What's wrong with a model that "relies on a constant stream of new money to pay earlier participants"?

解析：依赖新资金接盘，一旦断流就难以为继。Explanation: relying on new money for exit liquidity, it can't hold up once the flow dries up.

5. 判断收益模型是否可靠，正确做法是？5. What's the right way to judge whether a yield model is reliable?

解析：用数据面板和计算器核实真实储备与收入，自己判断。Explanation: use the dashboard and calculator to verify real reserves and income, and judge for yourself.

❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

收益全靠增发的项目一定是骗局吗？Is a project whose yield relies entirely on minting always a scam?

不能一概而论，但要高度警惕。增发本身会稀释价值，若没有真实收入和储备支撑，长期很难持续。关键看它有没有真金白银兜底，别只看年化。Not necessarily, but be very cautious. Minting itself dilutes value, and without real income and reserves to back it, it's hard to sustain long term. The key is whether there's real money backing it — don't just look at the APY.

怎么快速判断一个收益模型靠不靠谱？How can I quickly judge whether a yield model is trustworthy?

问三点:钱从哪来、储备里有没有真实资产、断了新资金还撑不撑得住。三点都心虚的，风险就很高。务必自己上链核实。Ask three things: where the money comes from, whether the reserves hold real assets, and whether it can hold up if new money stops. If all three feel shaky, the risk is high. Always verify on-chain yourself.

数字很漂亮的收益就能追吗？Can I chase a yield just because the numbers look great?

不能。高年化不等于净赚，币价一跌可能倒亏。数字只是表象，收益来源和真实储备才是本质，任何时候都要 DYOR、控制风险。No. A high APY doesn't equal net profit — if the token price drops you can end up in the red. The numbers are just the surface; the source of yield and the real reserves are what matter. Always DYOR and manage your risk.
