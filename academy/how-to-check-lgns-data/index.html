如何查看 LGNS 链上数据？How to check LGNS on-chain data?

⏱ 7 分钟7 min
学习目标：学完你能自己动手查 LGNS 的价格、供应、质押、国库等关键数据，不再依赖别人转述。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to look up LGNS's price, supply, staking and treasury data yourself, instead of relying on what others tell you.
大白话In plain wordsLGNS 的价格、发行量、质押率、国库这些数据全是公开的，你完全可以自己查、自己看，不用听别人说。本节教你几个上手的办法。LGNS's price, supply, staking ratio and treasury data are all public — you can look them up and read them yourself, no need to take someone's word for it. This lesson shows you a few hands-on ways to do it.

查 LGNS 链上数据，有三条互补的路子：There are three complementary ways to check LGNS on-chain data:

① 用本站首页「链上雷达」：一眼看到 LGNS 现价、24h 涨跌、成交量、流通量、质押率、国库市值、Anubis 链状态、近期成交与大额动态——都是实时从链上和公开接口读的，标注了数据来源。适合快速掌握全局。① Use the "On-chain Radar" on this site's homepage: at a glance you can see LGNS's current price, 24h change, volume, circulating supply, staking ratio, treasury market value, Anubis chain status, and recent trades and whale activity — all read in real time from the chain and public APIs, with the data source labeled. Good for quickly grasping the big picture.

② 用区块浏览器直接查：在 PolygonScan 输入 LGNS 合约 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01，能看到总供应、持有人、转账记录；查某个地址能看到它的 LGNS 余额和 sLGNS 质押量（记得 9 位精度要除以 10⁹）。Anubis 链的数据则用 Anubis 区块浏览器。② Query directly on a block explorer: on PolygonScan, enter the LGNS contract 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01 to see total supply, holders and transfer records; look up an address to see its LGNS balance and sLGNS staked amount (remember the 9 decimals — divide by 10⁹). For Anubis chain data, use the Anubis block explorer.

③ 用本站的链上工具：钱包体检（看某地址持仓/质押/授权/活动）、合约验证中心（看相关合约是否开源、权限）、链上证据库（关键事件带交易哈希）。把这些组合起来，你就能把「别人说的」变成「自己查到的」。③ Use this site's on-chain tools: Wallet Checkup (see an address's holdings / staking / approvals / activity), Contract Verify (see whether related contracts are open source and their permissions), Evidence DB (key events with transaction hashes). Combine these and you can turn "what others say" into "what you checked yourself".

核心心法：看到数字先问「这个数据是哪来的、能不能核实」。链上的东西都能点到浏览器一笔笔对，遇到只有口头、拿不出链上出处的说法，就要多留个心眼。Core mindset: when you see a number, first ask "where does this data come from, and can it be verified". Anything on-chain can be checked transaction by transaction on the explorer; when a claim is only spoken and can't point to an on-chain source, stay extra cautious.

案例Example有人说「LGNS 质押率 90% 以上」。你不用信也不用怀疑——打开本站链上雷达看质押率那一格，或者去查 sLGNS 的总供应对比 LGNS 总供应，自己算一遍就知道了。Someone claims "LGNS's staking ratio is over 90%". You don't have to believe it or doubt it — open this site's On-chain Radar and look at the staking-ratio tile, or compare sLGNS total supply against LGNS total supply and work it out yourself.

❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

普通人怎么自己查 LGNS 的链上数据？How can an ordinary person check LGNS on-chain data themselves?

三条路：用本站首页「链上雷达」看实时全局；用 PolygonScan / Anubis 浏览器直接查合约与地址（注意 9 位精度）；用本站钱包体检、合约验证、证据库等工具组合核实。Three ways: use this site's homepage "On-chain Radar" for a real-time overview; use PolygonScan / the Anubis explorer to query contracts and addresses directly (mind the 9 decimals); and combine this site's Wallet Checkup, Contract Verify and Evidence DB tools to cross-check.

查 LGNS 数据时最容易犯的错是什么？What's the most common mistake when checking LGNS data?

把 9 位精度当成 18 位，导致金额差一千倍；以及只听转述不核实。看到数字先问「哪来的、能不能核实」，再自己上链对。Treating the 9 decimals as 18, which makes amounts off by a thousand times; and taking hearsay without verifying. When you see a number, first ask "where's it from, can it be verified", then check on-chain yourself.
