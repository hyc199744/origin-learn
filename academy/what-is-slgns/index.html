sLGNS 是什么？What is sLGNS?

⏱ 5 分钟5 min
学习目标：学完你能说清 sLGNS 是质押凭证、它为什么会自动增长，以及它和 LGNS 的关系。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to say that sLGNS is a staking receipt, why it grows automatically, and how it relates to LGNS.
大白话In plain wordssLGNS 是你把 LGNS 质押后拿到的「存款单」，它会随着每 6 小时的 rebase 自动变多，代表你质押中的那份 LGNS。sLGNS is the "deposit slip" you get after staking LGNS. It grows automatically with each 6-hour rebase and represents the LGNS you have staked.

当你把 LGNS 质押进合约，会得到 sLGNS（staked LGNS）作为质押凭证。你可以把它理解成一张「存款收据」，证明你有多少 LGNS 正在质押。When you stake LGNS into the contract, you get sLGNS (staked LGNS) as a staking receipt. Think of it as a "deposit receipt" proving how much LGNS you have staked.

sLGNS 的特点是会自动增长：每 6 小时 rebase 一次，协议增发的奖励会体现为你的 sLGNS 余额变多。想退出质押时，用 sLGNS 换回 LGNS。大体上 1 sLGNS ≈ 1 枚已质押的 LGNS（含滚存的复利）。The key trait of sLGNS is that it grows automatically: it rebases every 6 hours, and the protocol's minted rewards show up as your sLGNS balance rising. When you want to unstake, you swap sLGNS back for LGNS. Roughly, 1 sLGNS ≈ 1 staked LGNS (including compounded rewards).

所以查一个地址「质押了多少 LGNS」，一个常用办法就是直接读它的 sLGNS 余额——那基本就是它当前的质押总量。So to check how much LGNS an address has staked, a common method is to read its sLGNS balance directly — that's basically its current total staked amount.

理性提醒：sLGNS 数量涨 = 你的质押份额在按增发变多，但这仍然是数量层面的增长。它值多少钱，取决于 LGNS 的币价。别把「sLGNS 变多」直接当成「赚了钱」。A reality check: a rising sLGNS count means your staked share is growing with minting, but that's still growth in quantity. What it's worth depends on the LGNS price. Don't treat "more sLGNS" as "made money".

案例Example你质押了 100 枚 LGNS，拿到约 100 sLGNS；几天后 sLGNS 变成了 102，说明这几天的 rebase 给你多滚出了约 2 枚 LGNS 的份额——但这 102 枚值多少钱，还得看当时的币价。You stake 100 LGNS and get about 100 sLGNS; a few days later the sLGNS becomes 102, meaning the rebases over those days rolled out about 2 more LGNS of share for you — but what those 102 are worth still depends on the price at that time.

📝 本节测验📝 Quiz

1. sLGNS 是什么？1. What is sLGNS?

解析：sLGNS 是质押 LGNS 后得到的凭证。Explanation: sLGNS is the receipt you get after staking LGNS.

2. sLGNS 为什么会自动变多？2. Why does sLGNS grow automatically?

解析：rebase 增发的奖励会让 sLGNS 余额自动增长。Explanation: rewards minted on rebase make the sLGNS balance grow automatically.

3. 1 sLGNS 大约等于？3. 1 sLGNS is approximately equal to?

解析：1 sLGNS ≈ 1 枚已质押的 LGNS（含滚存复利）。Explanation: 1 sLGNS ≈ 1 staked LGNS (including compounded rewards).

4. 想查某地址质押了多少 LGNS，一个常用办法是？4. To check how much LGNS an address has staked, a common method is?

解析：直接读 sLGNS 余额即约等于当前质押量。Explanation: reading the sLGNS balance directly gives approximately the current staked amount.

5. sLGNS 数量变多意味着？5. A growing sLGNS count means?

解析：数量增长不等于赚钱，价值取决于币价。Explanation: a growing count doesn't equal making money; value depends on the price.

sLGNS 和 LGNS 有什么区别？What's the difference between sLGNS and LGNS?
