Origin（起源）是什么？What is ORIGIN?

⏱ 6 分钟6 min
学习目标：学完你能说清起源是一整个生态，而不是一枚币，并知道它由哪几块组成。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to explain that ORIGIN is a whole ecosystem rather than a single coin, and know which parts make it up.
大白话In plain words起源（ORIGIN）不是一个币，而是一整套「链上社区 + 自己的代币 + 自己的公链 + 自己的治理」的组合，就像一个有自己货币和规则的数字国家。ORIGIN isn't a single coin — it's a whole combination of "on-chain community + its own token + its own public chain + its own governance", much like a digital nation with its own currency and rules.

起源（ORIGIN）是一整个 Web3 生态的统称，不是单一产品。它把几样东西拼在一起：一枚核心代币、一条自建公链、一套治理机制和一个社区。ORIGIN is the umbrella name for a whole Web3 ecosystem, not a single product. It stitches several things together: a core token, a self-built public chain, a governance mechanism, and a community.

核心几块：The core parts:

  • LGNS：生态的核心代币（算法代币，精度 9 位，不是 18）。LGNS: the ecosystem's core token (an algorithmic token, 9 decimals, not 18).
  • Anubis Chain：起源自建的公链（Chain ID 6714，gas 用 DAI 支付）。Anubis Chain: ORIGIN's self-built public chain (Chain ID 6714, gas paid in DAI).
  • GLGNS：治理代币，配合治理 NFT 用于社区投票。GLGNS: the governance token, used with governance NFTs for community voting.
  • Awake DAO：社区治理的地方，提案与投票在这里发生。Awake DAO: where community governance happens — proposals and voting take place here.

为什么要搞这么多块？因为起源想做的不只是「发一个币」，而是让代币、公链、金库、治理彼此咬合成一个可以自我运转的经济体。理解起源，就是把这几块的关系理顺。Why so many parts? Because ORIGIN aims to do more than "issue a coin" — it wants the token, the chain, the treasury, and governance to mesh into a self-running economy. Understanding ORIGIN means getting the relationships between these parts straight.

重要提醒：起源生态里很多说法（高收益、机制托底等）都需要你自己上链核实，本学院教你的正是「怎么自己查、自己判断」，而不是替你下结论。An important note: many claims in the ORIGIN ecosystem (high yield, mechanisms that "backstop" things, etc.) need you to verify them on-chain yourself. What this academy teaches is exactly "how to check and judge for yourself", not to draw conclusions for you.

案例Example当有人跟你说「起源就是 LGNS」，你现在可以纠正——LGNS 只是起源的核心代币，起源还包含 Anubis 公链、GLGNS 治理、Awake DAO 和国库，是一整个生态。When someone tells you "ORIGIN is just LGNS", you can now correct them — LGNS is only ORIGIN's core token. ORIGIN also includes the Anubis chain, GLGNS governance, Awake DAO, and the treasury; it's a whole ecosystem.

2. 起源的核心代币是哪一个？2. Which is ORIGIN's core token?

解析：LGNS 是起源生态的核心代币。Explanation: LGNS is the core token of the ORIGIN ecosystem.

3. 起源自建的公链叫什么？3. What is ORIGIN's self-built public chain called?

解析：Anubis Chain 是起源自建的公链。Explanation: Anubis Chain is ORIGIN's self-built public chain.

❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

起源和 LGNS 是一回事吗？Are ORIGIN and LGNS the same thing?

不是。起源（ORIGIN）是一整个生态的统称，LGNS 只是它的核心代币。起源还包含 Anubis 公链、GLGNS 治理代币、Awake DAO 和国库等部分。No. ORIGIN is the name for a whole ecosystem, and LGNS is only its core token. ORIGIN also includes the Anubis chain, the GLGNS governance token, Awake DAO, the treasury, and more.

起源生态由哪几部分组成？What parts make up the ORIGIN ecosystem?

主要是核心代币 LGNS、自建公链 Anubis Chain、治理代币 GLGNS 与治理 NFT、社区治理组织 Awake DAO，以及存放储备资产的国库。Mainly the core token LGNS, the self-built public chain Anubis Chain, the governance token GLGNS along with governance NFTs, the community governance body Awake DAO, and the treasury that holds reserve assets.
