Anubis Chain 是什么？What is the Anubis Chain?

⏱ 6 分钟6 min
学习目标：学完你能说清 Anubis 是起源自建的公链、它的 Chain ID，以及它最特别的「gas 用 DAI」是什么意思。Learning goal: after this lesson you'll be able to explain that Anubis is ORIGIN's self-built public chain, know its Chain ID, and understand what its most special trait — "gas paid in DAI" — means.
大白话In plain wordsAnubis 是起源自己搭的一条公链，最特别的是——在这条链上付手续费（gas）直接用 DAI，不用另外持有一种原生币。Anubis is a public chain ORIGIN built itself. The most special part: on this chain you pay the transaction fee (gas) directly in DAI, without needing to hold a separate native coin.

Anubis Chain是起源生态自建的公链。它的关键参数：Chain ID 6714，出块很快（约 1~2 秒一个块）。你可以在它的区块浏览器上查区块、交易和地址。Anubis Chain is a public chain built by the ORIGIN ecosystem itself. Its key parameters: Chain ID 6714, with fast block times (about one block every 1–2 seconds). You can look up blocks, transactions, and addresses on its block explorer.

它最与众不同的一点：gas（链上手续费）直接用 DAI 支付。在大多数链上你得先买一点原生币（比如以太坊上的 ETH）才能付手续费，而在 Anubis 上，你的原生余额就等于 DAI 余额，省去了「先换 gas 币」这一步。Its most distinctive trait: gas (the on-chain fee) is paid directly in DAI. On most chains you first have to buy some native coin (like ETH on Ethereum) before you can pay fees, but on Anubis your native balance equals your DAI balance, sparing you the "swap for gas first" step.

LGNS 也部署在 Anubis 上（合约 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8），所以起源是一个跨 Polygon + Anubis 两条链的生态。LGNS is also deployed on Anubis (contract 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8), so ORIGIN is an ecosystem spanning two chains, Polygon + Anubis.

提醒：自建链意味着这条链的很多规则由项目方设定，链上数据同样可以核实。想了解一条链是否活跃，可以看它的出块、地址数、交易数等公开指标（本站首页的「链上雷达」就实时展示了这些）。A note: a self-built chain means many of its rules are set by the project team, but its on-chain data can still be verified. To tell whether a chain is active, look at public metrics like its block production, address count, and transaction count (this site's homepage "On-chain Radar" shows these in real time).

案例Example你在以太坊上转账要先有 ETH 付 gas；但在 Anubis 上，你钱包里的 DAI 既是资产也是手续费，转账时直接扣 DAI，不用另外准备原生币。To transfer on Ethereum you first need ETH to pay gas; but on Anubis, the DAI in your wallet is both your asset and your fee — a transfer deducts DAI directly, with no separate native coin to prepare.
