Awake DAO 是什么？起源社区治理介绍

一句话：Awake DAO 是起源社区进行治理的地方——通过「提案 → 讨论 → 投票 → 执行」的流程做决策，投票权来自治理代币 GLGNS 与治理 NFT。

一、Awake DAO 治理核心事实

是什么起源生态的社区治理体系
决策流程提案 → 讨论 → 投票 → 执行
投票权来源GLGNS 治理代币（固定 21 万枚）+ 治理 NFT（双层）
与 LGNS 的关系治理用 GLGNS；LGNS 是核心代币，用于质押生息与交易

二、治理角色与决策流程

Awake DAO 的核心是让持有治理权的社区成员共同决定生态的重要事项。一个典型的流程：有人发起提案 → 社区讨论交换意见 → 进入投票 → 达到通过条件后执行。这套流程让生态的重要变更有据可循、可被核实。

三、投票权：为什么是 GLGNS 而不是 LGNS

这是新手最容易搞混的一点：治理投票权来自 GLGNS 治理代币和治理 NFT，不是直接拿 LGNS 或质押来投票。LGNS 是生态的核心代币（质押、生息、交易），GLGNS 才是专门用于治理投票的代币（固定 21 万枚、配双层 NFT 治理）。想参与治理，关注的是 GLGNS / 治理 NFT。

常见问题（FAQ）

Awake DAO 是什么？

Awake DAO 是起源（ORIGIN）社区进行治理的地方。社区通过提案、讨论、投票、执行的流程，对生态参数和方向做出决策。

Awake DAO 的投票权来自哪里？

投票权来自治理代币 GLGNS（固定 21 万枚 + 双层 NFT 治理结构），不是直接拿 LGNS 或质押来投票。也就是说，治理用 GLGNS，核心代币 LGNS 用于质押生息与交易。

一个提案怎么从发起走到执行？

流程是：提案 → 社区讨论 → 投票 → 执行。达到通过条件的提案，按规则进入执行。具体规则与门槛以官方公布为准，建议自己上链核实相关交易。

Awake 和 LGNS 的 rebase 参数有关系吗？

起源的一些生态参数（如质押 rebase 周期）属于治理/升级范畴，在 Awake 体系下调整。当前对外口径是每 6 小时 rebase 一次。

What Is Awake DAO? An Introduction to ORIGIN's Community Governance

In one line: Awake DAO is where the ORIGIN community carries out governance — decisions are made through a "propose → discuss → vote → execute" process, and voting power comes from the governance token GLGNS and governance NFTs.

1. Awake DAO Governance Core Facts

What it isThe community governance system of the ORIGIN ecosystem
Decision processPropose → discuss → vote → execute
Source of voting powerGLGNS governance token (fixed at 210,000) + governance NFTs (two-tier)
Relationship with LGNSGovernance uses GLGNS; LGNS is the core token, used for staking to earn and for trading

2. Governance Roles and the Decision Process

The heart of Awake DAO is letting community members who hold governance rights jointly decide on the ecosystem's important matters. A typical flow: someone raises a proposal → the community discusses and exchanges views → it goes to a vote → once the passing conditions are met, it is executed. This process makes important changes to the ecosystem traceable and verifiable.

3. Voting Power: Why It's GLGNS and Not LGNS

This is the point newcomers most easily confuse: governance voting power comes from the GLGNS governance token and governance NFTs, not from directly using LGNS or staking to vote. LGNS is the ecosystem's core token (staking, earning, trading), while GLGNS is the token specifically for governance voting (fixed at 210,000, with a two-tier NFT governance structure). If you want to take part in governance, what matters is GLGNS / governance NFTs.

FAQ

What is Awake DAO?

Awake DAO is where the ORIGIN community carries out governance. Through a process of proposing, discussing, voting, and executing, the community makes decisions on the ecosystem's parameters and direction.

Where does Awake DAO's voting power come from?

Voting power comes from the governance token GLGNS (fixed at 210,000 + a two-tier NFT governance structure), not from directly using LGNS or staking to vote. In other words, governance uses GLGNS, while the core token LGNS is used for staking to earn and for trading.

How does a proposal go from being raised to being executed?

The flow is: propose → community discussion → vote → execute. A proposal that meets the passing conditions proceeds to execution per the rules. The specific rules and thresholds are as officially announced; we recommend verifying the related transactions on-chain yourself.

Is Awake related to LGNS's rebase parameters?

Some of ORIGIN's ecosystem parameters (such as the staking rebase cycle) fall under governance/upgrades and are adjusted within the Awake system. The current stated figure is a rebase once every 6 hours.
