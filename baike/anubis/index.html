Anubis Chain 是什么？起源自建隐私公链完整介绍

二、核心机制：为什么 gas 用 DAI

大多数公链要求你先持有一个原生代币来付 gas（比如以太坊的 ETH、Polygon 的 POL）。Anubis 的做法不同——在协议层直接把 DAI 当作原生币，你的 DAI 余额既是资产也是 gas。好处是新手不用先去买一个陌生的 gas 代币，用稳定币就能直接在链上操作，体验更顺。

三、与 Polygon 的关系（双链结构）

起源生态同时运行在 Polygon 和 Anubis 两条链上：Polygon 是最早部署 LGNS 的链（2024-03-06），Anubis 是起源后来自建的公链（2026-04-08 主网）。LGNS、DAI 等核心资产两条链都有部署，可跨链使用。LGNS 在 Anubis 的合约地址是 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8。

What Is Anubis Chain? A Complete Guide to ORIGIN's Own Privacy Chain

In one line: Anubis is a privacy public chain built by the ORIGIN ecosystem — Chain ID 6714, ~2-second block time, gas paid directly in DAI, forming ORIGIN's dual-chain structure together with Polygon.

LGNS launch2026-05-13 (on the Anubis chain)

2. Core Mechanism: Why Gas Is Paid in DAI

Most public chains require you to first hold a native token to pay gas (for example ETH on Ethereum, POL on Polygon). Anubis does it differently — it treats DAI as the native coin at the protocol level, so your DAI balance is both your asset and your gas. The upside is that newcomers don't have to go buy an unfamiliar gas token first; you can operate on-chain directly with a stablecoin, for a smoother experience.

3. Its Relationship with Polygon (Dual-Chain Structure)

The ORIGIN ecosystem runs on both Polygon and Anubis: Polygon is the chain where LGNS was first deployed (2024-03-06), and Anubis is the public chain ORIGIN later built for itself (mainnet on 2026-04-08). Core assets such as LGNS and DAI are deployed on both chains and can be used cross-chain. LGNS's contract address on Anubis is 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8.

4. How to Use It / Add the Network

  • In your wallet's "Add Network" (e.g. MetaMask/OKX), enter: network name Anubis, RPC https://rpc.anubispace.org, Chain ID 6714, block explorer browser.anubispace.org.
  • Gas is paid in DAI, so keep a small amount of DAI in your wallet to send transactions.
  • All contracts and transactions can be verified on browser.anubispace.org, or use this site's "contract verification center" to view all Anubis-chain contracts.
⚠️ Security tip: when adding the network, only enter the official RPC and parameters — never ones given by an unfamiliar link; test with a small amount before bridging or trading, and verify on-chain yourself (DYOR).
