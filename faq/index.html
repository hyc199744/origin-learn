Origin / LGNS / Anubis 常见问题（FAQ）

关于 Origin、LGNS、Anubis Chain 最常被问到的问题，直接给答案，可在本站工具和区块浏览器自行核实。

Origin（起源）是什么？

Origin 是一整个 Web3 生态的统称，核心代币是 LGNS，并自建了 Anubis 隐私公链（Chain ID 6714）。它于 2024-03-06 在 Polygon 上线，采用源自 OlympusDAO 的 (3,3) 质押机制。

LGNS 是什么？

LGNS（朗基努斯）是 Origin 生态的核心代币——算法代币（不是稳定币），精度 9 位（不是 18），采用 (3,3) 质押模型、每 6 小时 rebase。部署在 Polygon 与 Anubis 两条链。治理用另一枚代币 GLGNS。

Anubis Chain 是什么？

Anubis 是 Origin 自建的隐私公链，Chain ID 6714，出块约 2 秒，gas 直接用 DAI（无需单独持有原生代币）。主网 2026-04-08 上线。

LGNS 合约地址是多少？

LGNS 在 Polygon 的合约地址是 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01；在 Anubis 的合约地址是 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8。均可在区块浏览器核实，也可在本站「合约验证中心」查看全部合约。

LGNS 的精度是多少？

9 位（decimals=9），不是常见的 18 位——这是 OlympusDAO 分叉代币的传统。链上原始数字除以 10⁹ 才是真实枚数。

LGNS 和 GLGNS 有什么区别？

LGNS 是核心代币，用于质押、生息、交易；GLGNS 是治理代币（固定 21 万枚 + 双层 NFT 治理），用于社区投票。治理用 GLGNS，不是 LGNS。

如何查询 LGNS 钱包余额 / 资产？

用本站的「钱包监控·链上体检」，输入任意地址即可看到它的 LGNS/sLGNS/DAI 等余额、交易记录、交互合约与授权风险。只读公开数据，不需要助记词或私钥。

如何查看链上交易记录？

把地址或交易哈希放进区块浏览器（Polygon 用 PolygonScan，Anubis 用 browser.anubispace.org），或用本站「钱包监控」看某地址的最近活动时间线。

LGNS 质押收益怎么算？

质押奖励本质是协议每 6 小时增发（rebase）新币，你的持仓数量会增加。用本站「收益计算器」输入本金和天数即可估算；但注意高年化不等于净赚，要同时看币价和真实储备。

LGNS 安全吗？合约能不能增发/升级？

是否安全应自己上链核实。用本站「合约验证中心」可查每个合约是否开源、是否可升级、Owner 是谁、有没有增发权限——这些都是链上公开信息。不要只听任何人一面之词。

什么是链上证据？

链上证据指真实存在于区块链上、可被任何人核实的记录：交易哈希、区块、合约地址、事件日志。本站「链上证据库」收录 Origin 关键事件，每条都附真实交易哈希——没有链上证据不进档案。

(3,3) 是什么？

(3,3) 是博弈论说法：如果大家都选择质押并长期持有，对所有人的整体结果最好（所以写成 3,3）。它用机制鼓励长期持有，但机制本身不保证赚钱。

怎么防止授权钓鱼（如 EIP-7702）？

不点陌生链接、不签看不懂的授权、定期检查并撤销钱包授权。收到「免费领空投/帮你找回被盗币」这类私信基本可当诈骗。真官方不会私信找你要助记词或让你转币。

Origin / LGNS / Anubis Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The most-asked questions about Origin, LGNS, and Anubis Chain — straight answers, all of which you can verify yourself with this site's tools and a block explorer.

What is Origin?

Origin is the umbrella name for an entire Web3 ecosystem. Its core token is LGNS, and it has built its own Anubis privacy chain (Chain ID 6714). It launched on Polygon on 2024-03-06 and uses the (3,3) staking mechanism derived from OlympusDAO.

What is LGNS?

LGNS (Longinus) is the core token of the Origin ecosystem — an algorithmic token (not a stablecoin), with 9 decimals (not 18), using the (3,3) staking model and rebasing every 6 hours. It is deployed on both Polygon and Anubis. Governance uses a separate token, GLGNS.

What is Anubis Chain?

Anubis is Origin's self-built privacy chain, Chain ID 6714, with roughly 2-second blocks, where gas is paid directly in DAI (no need to hold a separate native token). Mainnet launched on 2026-04-08.

What are the LGNS contract addresses?

On Polygon the LGNS contract address is 0xeB51D9A39AD5EEF215dC0Bf39a8821ff804A0F01; on Anubis it is 0x4D1D808a081FdAc440703b3765FC61f8028C06B8. Both can be verified on a block explorer, or you can view every contract in this site's "Contract Verification Center."

How many decimals does LGNS have?

9 (decimals=9), not the usual 18 — this is an OlympusDAO-fork convention. Divide the raw on-chain number by 10⁹ to get the real amount.

What's the difference between LGNS and GLGNS?

LGNS is the core token, used for staking, earning, and trading; GLGNS is the governance token (fixed at 210,000 + two-tier NFT governance), used for community voting. Governance uses GLGNS, not LGNS.

How do I check an LGNS wallet's balance / assets?

Use this site's "Wallet Monitor · On-chain Check-up": enter any address to see its LGNS/sLGNS/DAI balances, transaction history, contracts it has interacted with, and approval risks. It's read-only public data — no seed phrase or private key required.

How do I view on-chain transaction records?

Put the address or transaction hash into a block explorer (PolygonScan for Polygon, browser.anubispace.org for Anubis), or use this site's "Wallet Monitor" to see an address's recent activity timeline.

How is LGNS staking yield calculated?

The staking reward is essentially the protocol issuing (rebasing) new tokens every 6 hours, so your holding count grows. Use this site's "Yield Calculator," enter your principal and days to estimate; but note a high APY isn't net profit — you must also watch the price and the real reserves.

Is LGNS safe? Can the contract mint / upgrade?

Whether it's safe is something you should verify on-chain yourself. This site's "Contract Verification Center" lets you check whether each contract is open-source, whether it's upgradeable, who the Owner is, and whether it has minting rights — all public on-chain information. Don't just take anyone's word for it.

What is on-chain evidence?

On-chain evidence means records that genuinely exist on the blockchain and can be verified by anyone: transaction hashes, blocks, contract addresses, event logs. This site's "On-chain Evidence Library" records Origin's key events, each with a real transaction hash — no on-chain evidence, no entry in the archive.

What is (3,3)?

(3,3) is a game-theory notion: if everyone chooses to stake and hold long-term, the overall outcome is best for everyone (hence written as 3,3). It uses mechanism design to encourage long-term holding, but the mechanism itself does not guarantee a profit.

How do I avoid approval phishing (e.g. EIP-7702)?

Don't click unfamiliar links, don't sign approvals you don't understand, and regularly review and revoke wallet approvals. Direct messages like "free airdrop / we'll help recover your stolen coins" can basically be treated as scams. Genuine officials will never DM you asking for your seed phrase or telling you to transfer coins.
